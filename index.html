Presentation Builder

Generated from your material · click any text to edit it

How many slides? 6–10 for one topic — one idea per slide.

What to add, slide by slide:
1. Title — topic + your name & course
2. Introduction — definition & why it matters
3–5. Key concepts — one per slide, 4–6 short bullets
6. Diagram / worked example
7. Types / advantages
8. Exam focus — 2, 5 & 10-mark points
9. Summary — 3 key takeaways
10. References — your notes, syllabus, 2025 paper

Tips: ~6 words per bullet, large font (≥24pt), one visual per slide, high contrast, minimal text.

Images: Calpus adds a relevant illustration to every slide automatically — matched to the slide content.

Gemini/ChatGPT run via your local AI server (keys stay on the server, never in this page). Offline shows a template draft.
